FoodBuddies Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Last updated: March 12, 2026

FoodBuddies respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

Information We Collect

FoodBuddies may collect the following information when users create accounts or place orders:

  • name
  • email address
  • phone number
  • order history
  • payment information
  • device or usage data

This information helps FoodBuddies operate the platform and coordinate community food activities.

How We Use Information

FoodBuddies may use collected information to:

  • process food orders
  • coordinate group orders and pickup
  • communicate with users
  • improve the platform and community experience
  • provide customer support

Information Sharing

FoodBuddies may share necessary information with partner restaurants to fulfill orders.

FoodBuddies does not sell or rent personal data to third parties.

Payment Processing

Payment information may be processed by third-party payment providers.

FoodBuddies does not store full payment card information.

Data Security

FoodBuddies uses reasonable security measures to protect personal data.

However, no internet platform can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this Policy

FoodBuddies may update this Privacy Policy periodically.

Continued use of the platform indicates acceptance of updates.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at:

support@foodbuddies.community
